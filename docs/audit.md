# V4 scope and scientific audit

V4 extends the preserved, tested V3 source. The historical [V3 audit](audit-v3.md) records the original normalization, reward, task-boundary and topology issues. No repository was modified or deployed remotely.

## Requested experience and implementation response

- The topology studio was already implemented in V3 and is retained, with direct group selection, actual membership edits and undo/redo.
- “Neural” now refers to a real independent DQN implementation with gradient updates, replay, target copies, complete state migration and actual learning inspection. It does not rename the tabular learner.
- Behavioral controls instantiate stated numerical assumptions. Neutral defaults preserve the supported V3 model; their coefficients are not estimated human traits.
- Forecasting is an ensemble of actual engine continuations. Fresh generators and conditional checkpoint futures are labeled separately. Displayed winner counts, distributions, quantiles and Monte Carlo errors derive from saved runs.
- Calibration uses earlier aggregate observations only for parameter selection, evaluates later observations with disjoint seeds, and displays a simple baseline. Provenance and predictive validity remain unverified. The included example reports unfavorable predictive comparisons as well as favorable fits.
- User-defined objective weights and descriptive Pareto comparisons expose normative tradeoffs; no finite search is called a universal optimum.
- The handbook laboratory has representative coverage of all 19 chapters. Exact scope, reviewed sections, algorithm caps and omissions are listed before computation and in the coverage document. No claim is made to reproduce every experiment or theorem.
- Guided/Researcher disclosures, contextual dialogs and separated workspaces serve novice and advanced inspection without a single unbounded control screen.

## Important reductions

The dynamic society still has three policy alternatives, three issue axes and 12–600 actual agents. Choice-lab instances have separate bounded domains. A represented population only scales reporting. No LLM persona generation, empirical microdata model, neural centralized critic, PPO/MAPPO/SAC, learned political adversary, live election feed or perfect simulation is implemented. These limits are explicit in the UI and documentation.

Mathematical/algorithmic fixtures, exact replay, no-leakage checks, browser execution and ZIP extraction are engineering verification. They do not validate a human behavioral theory or an empirical electoral forecast. Final executed checks belong in verification.md.
